# Instagram and TikTok discovery for the APRILSKIN pilot

**Checked 2026-09-28.** Documentation investigation only; no accounts, grants, data collection, subscriptions or contact with creators. Scope: APRILSKIN Hero Cushion, sales in Japan, **both Instagram and TikTok**, **new-creator discovery and known-creator comparison**, solo/local-first, eventual USD 100/month total. Japanese-language relevance is acceptable; audience geography remains unknown unless separately evidenced.

**Resumed verification 2026-09-29:** the parent session read this saved artifact and independently checked the principal native-discovery capabilities, TikTok One's free search pricing, TikTok's business API product overview, the Accounts API's product-specific use restrictions, and Meta's marketplace availability announcement. This was documentation verification, not an account-access or data-acquisition test. Remaining endpoint and project-eligibility gaps below remain open.

**Finding:** both platforms offer official marketer-facing creator discovery. A manually assisted pilot is a plausible option, conditional on user acceptance and account access. An end-to-end automated route within the budget is **not established**. TikTok also documents business discovery APIs: the Display API's limits must not be mistaken for the limits of every TikTok product. Marketplace viewing, API access and permission to copy evidence into this application are separate questions.

## Policy boundaries and documentation gaps

TikTok's **Accounts API** expressly disallows extracting authorized creators' reports to build a separate affiliate influencer discovery/ranking program instead of using TikTok One or its API. This restriction belongs to that product; do not transplant it to all manually authored notes or claim every commercial comparison is forbidden. It makes “just connect creators' accounts” an unsafe assumption for this proposed route. [Accounts API permitted/prohibited uses](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/accounts-api-overview/v1.3).

TikTok One's current **Creative Services Advertiser Terms**, section 6(b), limit creator personal data obtained through those products to use in connection with those products. Confirm which terms apply to the actual marketplace/API account and whether the intended local shortlist use qualifies. This is a specific unresolved export/use question, not a finding that all internal shortlists are prohibited. [Current terms](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-one-terms-of-service?lang=en).

Meta endpoint and terms details remain an explicit gap after bounded direct reads, not evidence of permissive use. The official Postman overview establishes general capabilities only. Before ingestion, record the selected route's current scopes, storage/deletion requirements and permitted processing; do not invent a retention period or a blanket ban on scoring. No vendor was investigated deeply enough to establish both affordable minimum pricing and source/reuse authority. No vendor subscription is assumed in the budget.

## Conditional pilot path and decisions still needed

1. **Choose the acquisition workflow with the user:** either accept human-assisted native discovery plus local comparison, or require programmatic discovery at pilot entry. Both choices retain both platforms and both capabilities. The latter requires access validation of TikTok's business APIs and an Instagram discovery route before promising a sample; a known-creator-only import does not fulfill it.
2. **Validate the selected account route:** identify whose marketer/business account can access each marketplace and, if selected, whose app/account can obtain the necessary API grants. TikTok One region handling must expose the relevant Japanese creators; do not infer this from the user's residence. [Region boundary](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-working-with-creators-on-tiktok-one-campaigns?lang=en). Check current Meta marketplace/API access in the actual account. No registration or purchase has been authorized/performed by this research.
3. **Agree a small, observable sample:** proposed starting point, not an accepted quota: on each platform, three newly discovered candidates and two supplied known candidates, with two relevant recent posts per candidate where legitimately obtainable. Record discovery query/filter, date, source URL, origin as new/known, missing fields and whether evidence is observation, source metric or supplied record. Preserve platform-specific metrics rather than equating views or engagement across platforms.
4. **Test the decision the sample can support:** the marketer can assess Japanese-language relevance, cushion/complexion-product content fit and reasons for inclusion/exclusion. Without verified source analytics, record audience geography as **unknown**. Japanese text, creator location and follower count do not establish a Japanese buying audience. Content or engagement does not establish sales; historical sales claims need supplied campaign/order evidence and future sales evaluation needs an agreed measurement plan.
5. **Verify total cost and effort before implementation:** TikTok native discovery has a documented zero platform/search price; Instagram cost and API route costs are unresolved. Local comparison may fit the total USD 100 allowance, but access, permitted import, sample workload and model costs must be measured. Track human minutes separately: free search is not zero labor. No full affordability or product-fit claim is established by this note.
