# Affordable evidence access for a Japan beauty pilot

**Checked:** 2026-09-28. **Scope:** solo builder, no existing grants/accounts/datasets, local development, eventual **USD 100/month total operating budget**. Documentation research only: no registration, paid purchase, API acquisition or social dataset collection occurred. This resolves a feasibility inventory, not source selection or permission to collect. Existing [architecture research](../../proposal-research/research/03-data-and-technology-architecture.md) supplied leads; consequential claims below were rechecked against primary sources.

**Finding:** a small evidence workflow is plausible; broad automated Japanese-market Instagram/TikTok listening is not established. YouTube is the strongest documented public-discovery candidate, but text analysis and creator scoring have a consequential approval gate. An authorized/manual evidence workflow may fit sooner if the marketer needs Instagram/TikTok or verified audience geography. Product and search context cannot substitute for creator evidence. Choose between these routes after choosing the marketer's decision.

**Follow-up, 2026-09-29:** the user selected Instagram and TikTok. The [focused platform investigation](08-instagram-tiktok-discovery.md) adds their native creator marketplaces and TikTok's business discovery APIs, which were outside this initial Display API-focused inventory. Consult that investigation for the selected pilot; the earlier YouTube candidate is not a replacement for the user's platform choice.

## Material policy gates

**YouTube derivation permission is not automatic.** The base [Developer Policies, III.E.4.h](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies#refreshing,-storing,-and-displaying-api-data) restrict new/derived data and metrics. The [compliance guide, “Additional policies for derived metrics and data storage”](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies-guide) says the expanded policy applies to audited developers who explicitly apply and whose use case is approved. Its earlier “Acceptable metrics” section gives simple arithmetic and sorting examples; this is not a blanket license for scores or cross-platform composites.

The [additional policy, “Acceptable metrics” and “Data Storage”](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/derived-metrics-policy), updated **2026-09-14**, covers custom channel scores, content tagging, sentiment and creator-suitability planning under the amendment. The [actual application form](https://support.google.com/youtube/contact/yt_api_form?hl=en), Section 5 / Analytics & Reporting, requires agreement **and a qualification determination**, with product/privacy evidence. No such application or approval exists here. Therefore automated beauty tagging, sentiment, fit scores and ranking formulas must be scoped and cleared before promising them. Model inference being cheap or local does not establish permission; model-training rights were not established.

Source-provided fields displayed with attribution and a marketer's independently authored brief/decision are distinguishable from computed metrics. That distinction supports a narrower prototype; **human authorship or manual import does not itself bypass source restrictions**. Check whether proposed notes are actually derived from restricted API data. Keep independently supplied criteria visibly attributed and obtain clarification for ambiguous analytics use.

For public/non-authorized YouTube data, implement refresh/deletion within 30 days; owner-authorized statistical data has different conditions. User deletion requests have a seven-day limit. Expanded approval can allow certain statistics/derived metrics for 36 months, but not title/description/comment-text archives. Display source and freshness, and propagate removal into extracts and derived artifacts. Scraping, scraped-data procurement and audiovisual extraction are not fallback routes. [Developer Policies, III.E.4, III.E.6 and III.F.2](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies).

**Captions are a separate access problem.** YouTube's [caption download](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/captions/download) requires permission to edit the video and costs 200 quota units; a public video's visible subtitles do not establish download rights through that endpoint. For spoken-product analysis, use creator/brand-supplied original media or transcripts with a documented grant and source-policy check. Do not substitute an unlicensed transcript scraper.

**Rakuten's free access is not unrestricted reuse.** Its [terms](https://webservice.rakuten.co.jp/guide/rule), Articles 8(4), 10(4), 10(6) and 10(7), address linking, non-affiliate revenue, competing services and use/copying for permitted purposes. The English page expressly says the Japanese original governs. This research does **not** determine whether internal brand intelligence qualifies. Keep Rakuten optional until the governing terms, intended use and storage/derivation permission are resolved; begin product context with the marketer's authorized catalog if available.

**Instagram/TikTok remain gated.** Account consent does not alone establish every downstream reuse. Before API ingestion, record exact permission scopes, approved purpose, display rules, retention/deletion triggers and third-party model-processing permission. Unknown retention is not indefinite retention. The failed direct Meta reads are a specific unresolved check, not evidence of permissive terms.

## Explicit exclusions and interpretive limits

- **TikTok Research Tools:** unavailable to creators, advertisers and commercial users according to the [official FAQ](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/research-api-faq); a developer account alone is insufficient. Do not relabel this commercial project as academic research.
- **TikTok Commercial Content API:** documented countries are European, not Japan. It is not a route to Japanese organic beauty coverage. [Supported countries, updated 2026-09-01](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/commercial-content-api-supported-countries).
- **Paid listening suites, licensed social resellers and scraping marketplaces:** excluded from the initial experiment. No relevant approved license, current quote or affordable Japanese coverage was verified; this is not a claim that every vendor costs over $100. Do not buy a nominally cheap feed without checking its source authority and reuse rights.
- **Other marketplaces/review portals/search engines:** outside this bounded survey. Rakuten and Trends are the only supplementary routes assessed. No public review-text scraping is authorized by this inventory.

Japanese-language content, creator residence, market targeting and measured audience country are separate facts. YouTube `regionCode=JP` means viewable in Japan; `relevanceLanguage=ja` only favors Japanese relevance and can return other languages. Neither proves Japanese viewers. Search ordering/date filters can also yield incomplete samples. [Search parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/search/list). Audience-country evidence needs an appropriately authorized analytics report or creator-supplied report, with its period, denominator and provenance. YouTube Analytics documents a country dimension, including unknown country values. [Dimensions](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/dimensions#Geographic_Areas).

Source counts also change meaning: current YouTube documentation says that from **2026-08-24** `viewCount` counts starts across formats, including autoplay. Preserve metric definition/version and observation time; do not combine platform views into a comparable reach score. [Video statistics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos#statistics.viewCount).

## Small, cheap next experiment — conditional on the marketer decision

**Planning recommendation, not work already performed:** spend no money on data subscriptions initially. Allocate at most **$10/month to data acquisition as a proposed sub-budget**, leaving the rest of the $100 system ceiling for hosting, models and supporting services. This is a budget choice, not a vendor quotation.

1. Ask the marketer for one real decision, one category/brand set, 5–10 known creators and any legitimately shareable product/creator reports. Record an explicit grant and the provenance of imported fields; availability is currently unconfirmed.
2. If public discovery is sufficient, try one YouTube project after its policy/use-case assessment. Use five Japanese product/brand queries, two pages each, up to 50 results/page: **10 of the documented 100 daily search calls**, at most 500 result positions before deduplication. Enrich only about 100 selected videos and 10 creators, cap other reads at 50/day, retain only necessary fields. Recheck the actual Console quota. The current [quota table](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost), updated 2026-09-15, supersedes the old 100-units-per-search budgeting rule.
3. If Instagram/TikTok or audience-country evidence is essential, start with a rights-cleared import from a few consenting accounts; test one official connected-account integration only after setup feasibility is confirmed. Keep a manual source-link/evidence worksheet when automation is unavailable. Excerpts, screenshots, exports and transcripts still need their own permitted-use basis; an import button is not permission.
4. Produce 10 evidence cards and a 3–5 creator shortlist using the selected task's permitted fields. Add at most one Japan Trends CSV and a marketer-supplied product sheet. Defer automated tagging/sentiment/scoring until permissions and evaluation are settled.
5. Measure relevant-record yield, text availability, missing audience geography, duplicate rate, source freshness, marketer verification time and actual money/time spent. Stop if essential evidence remains unavailable or review/onboarding dominates the solo-builder budget. Compare the result with the marketer's current manual process before choosing the platform.

Every evidence row should retain `source`, `access_method`, `grant/policy_version`, `source_id/url`, `observed_at`, `published_at`, query/filter/rank where applicable, language, separately evidenced market/audience-country facts, missing-field reason, and refresh/deletion due date. These are project recommendations, not platform-required schema. Keep market/category configuration separate from source adapters for later global extension.
