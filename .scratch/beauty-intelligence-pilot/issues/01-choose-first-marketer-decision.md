## Question

For one real Korean beauty product or category targeting Japan, which recurring decision will the available brand marketer use this pilot to make, and what concrete output would improve their current way of making it?

Establish the product/category and campaign situation, intended audience, current manual process, and what a useful result looks like. Keep the integrated listening, shortlist, measurement-plan, and learning-record destination, while choosing the decision that anchors that loop. A campaign situation and current workflow must come from the human; recommendations and example scenarios do not count as their answer. Japanese-language reviewer responsibilities and translation needs belong to [Set pilot acceptance criteria and the first implementation boundary](07-set-acceptance-and-build-boundary.md); exact product-version and evidence boundaries belong to [Select the first live source and evidence scope](11-select-live-source-and-evidence-scope.md).

The access investigation makes two distinctions especially consequential: discovering new creators/signals versus comparing an existing candidate set, and Japanese-language relevance versus demonstrated Japanese audience share. Establish which evidence the marketer actually needs before selecting a platform.

## Answer

Resolved through the builder's live answers. The pilot serves the available APRILSKIN brand marketer deciding **which creators to shortlist for a sales-focused Hero Cushion campaign targeting Japan, and why each belongs on the shortlist**.

- Updated by the builder on 2026-09-29: TikTok is the first acquisition and pilot platform. The design must remain applicable to multiple platforms; Instagram is deferred from this first flow. This supersedes the earlier requirement to start with both platforms.
- Both discovery of new candidates and comparison of candidates already known to the marketer are required. The acquisition/automation method is still open.
- Japanese-language relevance is acceptable for an exploratory shortlist while audience geography is explicitly unknown. Language is not recorded as proof of audience country.
- The existing baseline is the marketer's intuition and ongoing monitoring. The intended improvement is an inspectable shortlist that makes supporting evidence, reasoning, and unknowns visible; measurable usefulness thresholds remain in the acceptance decision.
- The sales objective frames the campaign and later measurement plan. No creator sales forecast, verified conversion performance, demographic audience segment, or creator fee has been established.
- Hero Cushion is the product family; exact version/listing/shade scope remains to be set. The marketer's Japanese-language review ability and translation needs also remain open in the named downstream tickets.

The integrated listening, shortlist, measurement-plan, and learning-record destination remains intact.
