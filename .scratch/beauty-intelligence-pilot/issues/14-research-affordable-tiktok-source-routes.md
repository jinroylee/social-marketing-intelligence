## Question

Outside TikTok for Business developer access, what documented routes can a solo builder use to automate a small known-creator TikTok evidence sample in Japan within an eventual USD 100 monthly total operating budget? Compare officially supported customer/creator exports or embeds, permitted public-web collection, and licensed providers. Establish source terms, rights to process/retain evidence, actual minimum price and workload, obtainable fields, refresh cadence, and missing proof. Use first-party platform/provider sources only; do not buy, sign up, scrape, or claim live readiness. Write findings to `../research/14-affordable-tiktok-source-routes.md`.

## Answer

Documented routes, costs, fields, refresh limits, and source-rights gaps are recorded in [the cited findings](../research/14-affordable-tiktok-source-routes.md). Official TikTok oEmbed supports automated resolution of a supplied public URL to embed/attribution metadata, not machine-readable engagement or bulk creator posts. TikTok One project reports and selected Content Suite videos can be exported by a marketer with the required access; Content Suite needs an additional TikTok representative grant and brand profile. Bright Data advertises 5,000 free TikTok scraper records each month and USD 1.50/1,000 beyond; Apify has a cheaper-looking community Actor, but its platform plan is separate and its actor is neither Apify-maintained nor TikTok-authorized. Provider agreements leave TikTok source authorization and the client's retention/derivation rights unproven. No live route, account grant, sample return, or purchase was established. This resolves the documentation research only; [source selection](11-select-live-source-and-evidence-scope.md) remains a separate decision.
