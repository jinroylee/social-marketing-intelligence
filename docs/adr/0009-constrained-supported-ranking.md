# Constrain model ranking to supported criterion judgments

Status: accepted; implemented with simulated verification

The campaign owner permits a model to order one bounded set of deterministically eligible current-KB candidates. Grounding requires more than a citation ID beside arbitrary prose: such prose could invent audience, fees, availability or effectiveness while citing a real caption. The installed response therefore contains an exact candidate permutation and one constrained judgment per criterion, with citations limited to that candidate's exact supplied satisfied or contradicted criterion support. Unknowns retain a no-citation limited-evidence judgment. The server renders explanation templates; factual prose and authority-bearing fields invalidate the result.

This limits expressiveness to relative fit over the installed provisional caption criteria. It admits incomplete supported accounts, preserves all explicit gaps, distinguishes judgment from verified facts and avoids an additional model judge or human approval gate. Richer semantic assessment needs a separately justified evidence/validation contract rather than a looser schema.

One durable assessment claim pins inputs and owns retries before provider dispatch. No transaction spans I/O. Shared monetary admission and reported consumption remain with the existing accounting owner. A campaign-owned exact-input guard reopens every prepared candidate copy and its process/display/retention, derivation and required-interpretation health after accounting contention, retaining source locks through dispatch commit. Refusal has no model call or monetary liability and remains a durable blocked attempt on retry. Finalization repeats full input checks and both campaign heads before automatic shortlist publication. Empty batches save a no-call outcome. Failed, stale or interrupted assessments remain visible without a successful deterministic fallback or blind paid retry. A lost final save can leave a paid claim in progress; recovering a missing response is separate work and cannot be inferred from cost reconciliation.

Governed assessed input and judgment copies share candidate support dependencies, current-policy hiding and retention purge. Permitted membership/order and source-free provenance remain pinned, and retries never reconstruct purged explanations. This preserves [automatic save authority](0003-automatic-shortlist-persistence.md) and [current-policy support](0002-pinned-support-current-policy.md) while making the present analytical limit explicit.
