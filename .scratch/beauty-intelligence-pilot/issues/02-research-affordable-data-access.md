## Question

Starting with no accounts, datasets, licenses, or customer grants established, which obtainable data routes could support Japanese-language beauty listening and evidence-backed creator comparison within an eventual total system budget of about USD 100 per month?

Investigate a bounded set of plausible sources from current primary documentation. Distinguish public discovery from access to an authorized account's own information, an API's documented existence from access granted to this project, and Japanese text from verified Japanese audience reach. Cover setup requirements, fees or quotas, obtainable fields, material storage/display/derivation restrictions, sampling limits, and information that will remain unknown. Include a manual/imported evidence fallback when automated acquisition is unavailable. State whether product/review sources can supplement context without providing creator evidence. Recommend candidates for a small acquisition experiment; do not choose the pilot's final platform on the human's behalf, sign up, or purchase access.

Write findings to `../research/02-affordable-data-access.md` and link the artifact in the resolution. Reuse the existing proposal research as leads, verifying consequential claims against current official documentation.

## Answer

Resolved on 2026-09-28 at the level of documented routes and access conditions. YouTube is a candidate for a small public-discovery experiment, while official Instagram/TikTok routes depend on appropriate accounts, grants, and approved uses. Supplementary product/search sources cannot establish creator suitability or Japanese audience reach. YouTube's additional requirements for derived analytics are material to the proposed tagging, sentiment, and scoring workflow; inexpensive API access alone does not settle feasibility.

The [data-access findings](../research/02-affordable-data-access.md) contain the official source inventory, current quotas, restrictions and unresolved checks, and a bounded acquisition experiment. The parent session independently checked the consequential YouTube quota and derived-metrics documentation. No dataset was acquired, account access granted, source selected, or whole-loop feasibility demonstrated. The investigation is complete; access onboarding and a real sample remain subsequent work.

The next choices are already covered by [Choose the first decision with the brand marketer](01-choose-first-marketer-decision.md) and [Select the first live source and evidence scope](11-select-live-source-and-evidence-scope.md): discovery versus comparison of known creators; language relevance versus demonstrated audience geography; available marketer/creator grants; and whether automated scores are essential. Source-specific prerequisite tickets can be created after that choice.
