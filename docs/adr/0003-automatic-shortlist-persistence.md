# Allow automated shortlist persistence without human review

Status: accepted; deterministic and bounded model persistence implemented

An agent's ranked recommendation may be saved as a campaign shortlist without human review, confirmation or approval. Requiring a human click merely to preserve an internal recommendation would obstruct the requested workflow; persistence does not execute a campaign or assert that a person endorsed the result.

Record automated or human authorship, ranking/method versions, ordered account membership, rationale, gaps and pinned support. Optional human curation/review is distinct from saving and must not be fabricated by an automation actor. Discovery completion offers regeneration rather than replacing saved results. Assistant-origin external enrichment still requires its own approval, and chargeable work remains subject to spending controls.

The local store implements this authority through `CampaignStore.recommend`: a current-KB deterministic comparison and its ordered `generated` shortlist commit together with actual automation attribution and version checks. Exact retries reopen the original without reassessment. Human draft/confirmation operations retain their distinct human-only contract, and migration preserves their historical status and unrecorded ordering. `CampaignStore.recommend_ranked` additionally saves validated bounded model order after deterministic eligibility, with durable claims, shared monetary admission and final support/head fencing. No transaction spans provider I/O; failed/interrupted attempts remain visible without reissue or a successful fallback. [Constrained judgments](0009-constrained-supported-ranking.md) explain the grounding boundary. Richer optional curation remains [target work](../functional-requirements.md).
