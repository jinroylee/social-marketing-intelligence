# Official TikTok routes for public Japan creator evidence

**Checked 2026-09-29.** This is documentation research for a commercial Japan beauty-marketing pilot that must eventually compare creators who have not authorized the app. I read TikTok's current developer documentation and terms, and fetched the official Japanese-language terms page as a legal document. I did not collect creator content, use an undocumented endpoint, create an account, obtain a grant, or run a scraper. Documentation is not proof of live access or use rights.

## Decision-relevant finding

No documented regular, non-Business TikTok developer endpoint found in the reviewed product surface returns an arbitrary unconsenting Japan creator's post history or engagement counts for this commercial use. TikTok's documented **oEmbed** endpoint can resolve a *known* public video or profile URL to attribution and embed markup, but its JSON is not a structured creator-performance feed. Direct automated collection from TikTok's ordinary website conflicts with the currently served Japanese-language *Other Regions* terms text, which prohibits automated scripts collecting information from or interacting with the service. The page identifies its last update as February 2020; the account-specific contract and any later changes should be checked before a production collector decision. [Video oEmbed](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-videos); [profile oEmbed](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-creator-profiles); [Japanese Other Regions terms, §5](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/terms-of-service/ja).

## Japanese web-collection terms and the oEmbed boundary

On 2026-09-29, `https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/terms-of-service/ja` returned HTTP 200 with a Japanese document headed “Other Regions” and “last updated February 2020.” Section 5 forbids using automated scripts to collect information from, or otherwise interact with, the service; the same section restricts commercial or unauthorized use absent TikTok's express written consent and bars bypassing access controls. This is stronger evidence against building a direct profile-page scraper than the previous Traditional-Chinese cache, because it is the Japanese-language page fetched directly from TikTok today. Its 2020 revision date and broad “Other Regions” label leave a residual question about the exact terms presented to this builder's TikTok account; they do not turn website scraping into an authorized route. [Japanese Other Regions terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/terms-of-service/ja). A [TikTok Shop Japan product term](https://seller-jp.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3127057786734353&lang=ja-JP) also refers to the platform provider in TikTok's Other Regions service terms, which supports—but does not conclusively establish—the regional mapping for this builder.

The official [video](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-videos) and [profile](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-creator-profiles) embed guides explicitly allow calling `/oembed` for a supplied URL. That narrowly documented programmatic operation should be distinguished from extracting creator metrics out of ordinary page HTML, player internals, or rendered embed markup. The embed guides describe display and attribution; they do **not** state a permission to build a commercial historical creator database, a retention period, a bulk refresh quota, or a license to copy video/media. A URL resolving successfully is technical access, not commercial processing permission. [Video embed guide](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-videos); [profile embed guide](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-creator-profiles).

TikTok's [Developer Terms, definitions and §II.1](https://t.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tik-tok-developer-terms-of-service/en) define developer services broadly and allow automated collection **only as described in developer documentation**. Sections III.3(c) and III.3(h) restrict unconsented commercial use and building profiles/databases on individuals; §VI requires deleting TikTok Information obtained through developer services upon termination. The terms text does not explicitly classify this no-key oEmbed call or grant an exception for storing its response in a marketer-facing intelligence database. **Inference:** a small URL-to-embed connector stays closest to the published oEmbed purpose if it stores only operational URL-resolution provenance needed for an ephemeral/local validation and presents TikTok content through the live embed with attribution. Durable commercial scoring/retention of returned title, author, thumbnails, or extracted rendered metrics needs TikTok-specific use-rights clarification. This is a product-risk reading of the published text, not a legal determination. [Developer Terms](https://t.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tik-tok-developer-terms-of-service/en); [video embed guide](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-videos).

## What remains unverified

The Japanese account's actually accepted/current terms, a TikTok grant for this commercial analysis, a license or retention schedule for oEmbed-derived metadata, production oEmbed rate/cost behavior, and any source capable of lawful recurring engagement refresh for unconsenting Japan creators remain unverified. A successful URL-resolution sample must not be labeled a comparison-grade live source or evidence of sales, Japan audience, post engagement, or discovery. [Video oEmbed](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-videos); [Developer Terms](https://t.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tik-tok-developer-terms-of-service/en).
