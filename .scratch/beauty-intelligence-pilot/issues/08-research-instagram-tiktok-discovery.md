## Question

The earlier access inventory did not settle Meta's endpoint limits/terms or a general TikTok discovery route. Now distinguish platform-native marketer discovery interfaces, official programmatic discovery, known-account lookups, consenting-account data, and imports of obtainable evidence. Check official creator marketplace/marketing tools as well as developer APIs; native search being available to a human does not imply an automatable API. If a documented vendor route is considered, establish its actual minimum price and source/reuse basis rather than inferring feasibility from a trial or vendor marketing claim.

Use current primary sources to identify minimum accounts/grants, fields and content types, commercial-use limitations, fees/quotas where documented, and unresolved verification. Identify what a realistic first sample can and cannot demonstrate about product fit, Japanese-language relevance, audience geography, and sales-related evidence. The user's selection of both discovery and known-candidate comparison must be preserved; manually assisted discovery is an option to put to the user, not an assumed substitute for their requirement. Do not acquire data, create accounts, purchase services, or contact anyone.

Write findings to `../research/08-instagram-tiktok-discovery.md`. Resolve the documentation investigation separately from selecting a route or proving actual project access.

## Answer

Resolved on 2026-09-29 after resuming and verifying the research artifact saved on 2026-09-28. Both platforms document marketer-facing discovery interfaces. TikTok One documents free native creator search and TikTok's business API products include creator discovery; the Display API's absence of search is not a platform-wide absence of discovery APIs. Instagram's native marketplace and general professional-account APIs are separate routes with different capabilities.

A manually assisted first pilot is a conditional option preserving both new-candidate discovery and known-candidate comparison on both selected platforms. A fully automated route within the total USD 100 monthly budget has not been established. Actual account eligibility, app permissions, permitted evidence transfer, API fees/quotas, and an obtained sample remain unverified. Connecting a brand account does not by itself grant all marketplace or discovery API capabilities.

The [focused discovery findings](../research/08-instagram-tiktok-discovery.md) hold the capability matrix, primary sources, product-specific restrictions, remaining gaps, and a proposed small sample. No source was connected, dataset acquired, service purchased, or route selected. [Select the first live source and evidence scope](11-select-live-source-and-evidence-scope.md) must establish available account access and decide the required level of discovery automation before specific onboarding/sample tasks are created.
