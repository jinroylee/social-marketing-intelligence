# Social Marketing Intelligence

Shared language for evidence-backed market investigation and creator campaign decisions.

## Language

**Brand**:
The commercial identity under which products are marketed. A brand is distinct from the company that owns or operates it.

**Target market**:
The geographic market a campaign intends to address. It is distinct from a product's origin, a post's language, and the measured location of a creator's audience.

**Market monitoring**:
Ongoing observation and investigation of accessible market evidence that informs marketers and the shared knowledge. It operates independently of campaign initiation and creator selection.

**Market signal**:
An evidence-backed observation or interpretation of a pattern or change in accessible market activity, with its time window and coverage limits. A signal can inform a marketer's understanding without initiating a campaign.

**Monitoring investigation**:
A saved topic or product question with an evidence summary and explicit sample limits. An investigation can describe a fixed snapshot without establishing market growth or change over time.

**Market insight**:
A versioned finding or summary answering a monitoring investigation, with its supporting evidence, relevant times, and coverage limits. It may inform a campaign brief without initiating a campaign.

**Campaign**:
An internal planning context created by a marketer, described by a campaign brief and associated decision records. Its creation does not mean that an external campaign has been activated.

**Campaign brief**:
A marketer-defined statement of the brand/product scope, objective, target market, platforms, and at least one creator-selection criterion. It is the starting point for campaign planning and exploratory creator comparison even when commercial details remain unknown.

**Creator**:
A person, group, or organization that creates content, distinct from any particular platform account. Accounts are linked to a creator through corroborated evidence or a recorded marketer correction.

**Creator shortlist**:
A campaign's selected candidate platform accounts, optionally associated with corroborated creator identities, with the available evidence and reasons for considering each. Inclusion does not mean the creator has agreed to participate or has been contracted.

**Creator discovery**:
Finding candidate creators who are not already in the marketer's working set for a campaign. Discovery produces candidates for evaluation; it does not establish their suitability or availability.

**Creator comparison**:
Evaluating candidate creators against the same campaign needs using their available evidence and explicit unknowns. The candidates may come from discovery or the marketer's existing working set.

**Comparison draft**:
A provisional comparison of candidate accounts against an exact campaign brief and evidence set, with supported judgments, reasons, and unknowns. Preparing it does not confirm the selected shortlist.

**Shortlist revision**:
A saved version of a campaign's selected candidate accounts and decision basis. A confirmed revision records the marketer's selection and its supporting evidence; later changes create a successor revision.

**Platform account**:
An identity on a particular source platform under which content and account metrics are observed. It is distinct from the creator associated with it and from its mutable display name or handle.

**Source reference**:
A supplied or discovered pointer to possible evidence, such as a content URL or account handle. A reference does not establish that the resource was retrieved, is available, or belongs to a verified identity.

**Source observation**:
Evidence obtained through a particular acquisition route, with its source, acquisition context, relevant times, coverage, and permitted uses. An observation is distinct from the real-world account or content it describes; several observations may describe the same entity.

**Assertion**:
An observed, derived, or human-supplied claim about an entity, retaining its evidence and relevant context. Conflicting assertions can coexist; a preferred current value does not erase the conflict or make missing evidence certain.

**Metric observation**:
A measurement with its metric definition, value, subject, relevant time or window, and source evidence. The same label from different sources does not by itself establish comparable measurements.
