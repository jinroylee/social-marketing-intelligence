# Reconcile named cost portions and recover meter health separately

Status: accepted

Delayed synthetic billing must reduce the correct outstanding liability without erasing the original declaration, uncertainty or dispatch evidence. The accounting owner records an immutable final-evidence reconciliation naming its exact operation-bound reservation and/or entry identities, together with one final usage measurement and a human operator's source-free report attestation. The scope lock serializes this settlement with admissions and independent ledger writes. A final bill can be allocated once; changed reports or overlapping settlements conflict before creating another charge. Existing equal usage can be linked while preserving its recorder. Actual measurements retain their original rate, currency and period even when the result exceeds an advertised bound or configured ceiling.

Timeout release would mistake an ambiguous outcome for free work. Replacing declarations in place would lose the reason an allowance was previously consumed. Applying every late report additively would count one final bill twice. We instead retain the audit entries and derive active commitments/uncertainty from explicit resolutions. A confirmed uncharged failure requires a final zero measurement; failure status alone cannot release a bound. No automatic expiration or bounded write-off policy is installed. The trusted operator's synthetic evidence is an assertion, not independent verification of commercial billing.

A failed expected meter has two distinct obligations: account for the affected cost and establish that future metering is healthy. Final usage can settle the former while the entire scope remains blocked. A separate human recovery record identifies already-reconciled failures and an explicit health-check attestation; only those failures are cleared. Increasing a ceiling, receiving known usage or changing a task's status cannot substitute for recovery. A final report that disproves its dispatch bound records its own expected-meter failure, with cost resolved by that same report and health still requiring recovery. New or unnamed failures still block work. Scope classification remains immutable rather than falling back to forecasts.

Accounting resolution never restores a transient response or grants another dispatch. Reconciled reservations remain settled; original owned callbacks reuse equal final units and reject conflicting units. Task ownership sessions still fence their local commits, and publication recovery still requires its existing ownership receipt and source lineage. A task may stay waiting after accounting is final. Live-provider evidence verification, provider-side idempotency, response recovery and any future bounded policy need their own explicit contracts. [The accounting contract](../costs.md#final-synthetic-reconciliation-and-meter-recovery) describes the installed operations and visible lag/enforcement limits.
