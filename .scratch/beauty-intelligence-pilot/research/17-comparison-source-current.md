# Comparison-grade TikTok source: current public evidence

**Checked:** 2026-09-30. **Question:** Can this local-first Japan pilot automatically refresh profile and recent-post metrics for known, public TikTok creators who have not authorized the app, within a future USD 100/month *total* operating budget? This is documentary research only. No provider account, paid service, scraper, creator-content fetch, or provider contact was used. “Vendor claim” means a provider's published product description, not a verified response or a TikTok grant. The earlier [regular-access](13-regular-tiktok-developer-access.md), [affordable-route](14-affordable-tiktok-source-routes.md), and [Japan-route](15-japan-public-tiktok-routes.md) notes provide additional context.

## Decision-relevant result

**No comparison-grade route is yet verified end to end.** Bright Data and a community Apify Actor advertise the needed public profile and post outputs at prices that could fit the pilot. Neither public contract shown below establishes TikTok's authorization for the underlying collection or this project's right to retain a historical creator database, display evidence to marketers, delete on source changes, and send source text to an LLM. Neither route has been tested on the supplied Japanese creator. Low advertised cost establishes only a candidate for a bounded *authorized* test. [Bright Data TikTok product](https://brightdata.com/products/web-scraper/tiktok), [Bright Data agreement](https://brightdata.com/license), [Apify Actor terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/actor-terms-and-conditions), [TikTok Japanese Other Regions terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/terms-of-service/ja).

TikTok's documented no-consent [profile oEmbed](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-creator-profiles) returns attribution and embed HTML for a supplied URL, while the follower counters and recent videos are features of the **rendered card**, not fields in the documented JSON. The [Display API](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/display-api-overview) reads a consenting creator's videos. The [Research Tools FAQ](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/research-api-faq) explicitly answers “No” for creators, advertisers, or commercial users seeking Research Tools access. The [Commercial Content API](https://developers.tiktok.com/products/commercial-content-api) currently includes EU ads/content, not a Japan-wide ordinary creator feed. These official routes do not close this ticket's arbitrary-unconsenting-creator requirement. TikTok's [Accounts API documentation](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/accounts-api-overview/v1.3) also expressly prohibits using authorized account reports to build a self-run influencer discovery/ranking program in place of TikTok One; the builder lacks Business API access anyway.

## Rights, retention, deletion, display, and model use

- **Collection provenance.** Bright Data describes itself as extracting from TikTok's website, and the Apify route is a community-hosted public-web Actor. Neither page provides a TikTok source agreement or a TikTok-authorized endpoint. TikTok's currently served [Japanese-language Other Regions terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/terms-of-service/ja) prohibit automated scripts collecting information from the ordinary service; that page is dated February 2020, so the exact account-specific/current contract and the legal effect on a vendor need specialist review. This note does **not** conclude that every public-data collection method is unlawful; it concludes that source permission for this system has not been demonstrated. [Bright Data product](https://brightdata.com/products/web-scraper/tiktok), [Apify Actor](https://apify.com/zapticx/tiktok-profile-intelligence), [prior Japan-route source reading](15-japan-public-tiktok-routes.md).
- **Retention and deletion.** Bright Data's agreement says it may cease holding delivered dataset exports after a provider-side review period, and may retain/use data it collects for its own purposes; it does not state how long this client may store TikTok records or how it should purge removed posts and derivatives. [Agreement §10](https://brightdata.com/license). Apify says unnamed datasets expire after seven days unless otherwise specified, and documents a dataset deletion API, but those platform storage controls do not define this client's TikTok rights or derived-data duties. [Dataset storage](https://docs.apify.com/storage/dataset), [dataset API](https://docs.apify.com/api/v2/storage-datasets), [Actor terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/actor-terms-and-conditions).
- **Display and LLM use.** Bright Data's product advertises competitive analysis and agent integration, while Apify's Actor lists AI workflows; those marketing statements do not specify whether this project may display captions/counts to a brand marketer, send captions to a third-party LLM, train a model, or keep extracted assertions after a source post disappears. Bright Data markets separate AI datasets for training, but that is not a TikTok-specific license for this scraper product. Treat each use separately until the relevant provider/source terms or written grant address it. [Bright Data TikTok page](https://brightdata.com/products/web-scraper/tiktok), [Bright Data AI datasets](https://brightdata.com/products/datasets/ai), [Apify Actor](https://apify.com/zapticx/tiktok-profile-intelligence), [Bright Data agreement](https://brightdata.com/license).

## Smallest test that would change the decision

Before enabling either vendor connector, obtain a reviewable written statement or applicable agreement that identifies **the actual TikTok acquisition route and its authority**, and explicitly covers this pilot's internal creator comparison, retention period, removal/deletion propagation, marketer-facing displays, derived metrics, and sending text to the selected LLM (including whether model training is forbidden or allowed). Confirm whether an individual solo developer can open the account and the exact free-tier/billing conditions. A provider's general privacy-compliance claim is not a substitute. [Bright Data agreement](https://brightdata.com/license), [Apify general terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/general-terms-and-conditions), [Apify Actor terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/actor-terms-and-conditions).

If that gate is met and the builder authorizes account creation/test usage, run **one** bounded Japan sample: the already supplied `@maronayannyan` profile/video plus two marketer-selected public Japan beauty creators, at most five recent posts per profile and one refresh after a known interval. Pre-set a small hard spend cap; retain only the minimum test evidence allowed by the grant. Record returned creator/post IDs and links, field presence and types, timestamps, whether the supplied video is linked to the right creator, post ordering and window, duplicates, photo handling, missing/partial/private/deleted outcomes, latency, request/result counts and actual invoice/credit usage. Separate *creator location or audience-country evidence* from interface locale or proxy country. Compare against a human review of the live TikTok pages only within the agreed test scope. If the grant cannot be obtained, keep the current oEmbed reference adapter and use explicitly provided marketer/creator evidence for a narrowed pilot; do not describe that as automatic unconsenting-creator refresh. [Bright Data product](https://brightdata.com/products/web-scraper/tiktok), [Apify Actor listing](https://apify.com/zapticx/tiktok-profile-intelligence), [TikTok profile oEmbed](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-creator-profiles).

**Unresolved after public research:** account eligibility, written source authority, Japan success rate/coverage, data recency, exact per-run maximum and request cost, client retention and derived-data rules, deletion behavior, downstream display/LLM use, real sample schema, and whether any provider will give these assurances within the project's total budget. This ticket still needs the builder's explicit scope/risk choice; the research alone does not select a production source.
