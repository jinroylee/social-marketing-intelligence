# Independent monitoring

[`smi_monitoring`](../src/smi_monitoring/__init__.py) owns human investigation scopes and versioned, rule-based insight revisions. It consumes public knowledge/evidence interfaces and has no campaign dependency or campaign-creation action.

Snapshot investigations and [bounded offline recent-X evidence](#bounded-offline-recent-x-evidence) are separate implemented paths. One [configured quantitative volume assessment](#configured-quantitative-volume-assessments) now retains supported alerts and local notifications. One [bounded provisional quality-controversy method](#bounded-provisional-quality-controversy-assessments) also retains semantic alerts. Semantic shift, broader controversy analysis and KB-based explanation in the [target anomaly flow](functional-requirements.md#c--lightweight-x-monitoring-and-anomaly-detection) remain future work; snapshot preparation is independent.

## Scope and preparation

`InvestigationScope` requires a catalog, question, intended market context, platforms and at least one Brand/Product/topic reference. Market context describes human intent, not audience geography. Publication bounds use timezone-aware timestamps. See the [example scope](../examples/monitoring/hero-cushion-scope.json).

`MonitoringStore.start(scope, human_actor, request_id=...)` creates the first scope. `revise_scope(..., expected_version=..., request_id=...)` appends a successor. `prepare_insight(investigation_id, scope_id, knowledge_run_id, actor, expected_version=..., request_id=...)` pins the current scope, exact knowledge run/catalog, dataset ID/digest, actual observations, method/version, actor and time. Human or automation contexts can prepare answers; scope writes require a trusted human.

The method is `rule_based`, `caption-snapshot-summary-v1`. It filters processable, retainable, current observations by platform/publication time. Any selected reference may match. Within matched posts, it summarizes requested mentions/topics and literal PR/Qoo10 markers, retaining exact interpretation/assertion/span dependencies.

Counts use distinct content identities and exact namespace/native account keys. Repeated captures are not extra posts. A PR marker is not a paid contract; Qoo10 wording is not a conversion; family mentions are not exact variants. Findings remain provisional and sample-scoped, without stance, efficacy, audience or sales inference.

Caption language is an explicit optional constraint. The current method establishes script only, so requiring a language returns `language_constraint="not_assessable"` and no asserted language-filtered subset. Leave it empty for literal-scope retrieval without a language claim. An empty window/reference result means “no matching observations in this dataset,” not market absence.

## Revisions and policy

Separate scope/insight heads are serialized under an investigation lock. Expected versions reject competing edits; identical request retries return the original record. Changed inputs/actors under the same ID conflict. A fresh answer uses a new request; earlier answers remain tied to their original scope. `list_investigations`, `read_investigation` and `read_insight` reopen history across restarts.

Coverage records original requested coverage, scanned/matched observation IDs, language status and `baseline=null`. `trend_status="not_assessable"` prevents snapshot findings from becoming growth/change alerts. Snapshot preparation does not run the separate recent-window volume method; no recurring scheduler is implemented.

Commit-time source/derivation locks recheck finding dependencies and surviving knowledge support; withdrawal/correction causes `insight_support_changed` and rolls back. Reads hide denied finding statements/counts/excerpts; superseded support requires review. Retention withdrawal, expiry or revocation purges finding bodies while preserving permitted IDs, original membership and attribution. Permission recovery or identical retries cannot reconstruct them.

Redaction is conservative per finding: one withdrawn dependency withholds its aggregate rather than silently recomputing history. Matching membership/counts are also withheld (`actual_observation_ids=null`) while a finding is unavailable, so coverage cannot reproduce a hidden aggregate. Original stored audit membership remains pinned. A successor can summarize still-permitted evidence. Reconciliation is synchronous; no background deletion or universal file cleanup is claimed.

## Browser workflow

`/investigations` is independently reachable in the [local review application](local-review.md). Create/revise a scope, choose a dataset, prepare an insight and expand findings for original captions, highlighted support, source/date lineage, capture age and limits. Scope/answer history is retained. Preparation makes no source/provider/model call; translations and language adjudication remain pending until supplied.

## Bounded offline recent X evidence

[`smi_monitoring.recent`](../src/smi_monitoring/recent/__init__.py) owns `RecentMonitoring` and `OfflineXRoute`. It installs independent tables with `migrate()`; the existing `MonitoringStore.migrate()` also installs this additive schema. Existing investigation IDs, frozen source membership, snapshot answers and form protections retain their original meaning. Recent collection requires no catalog, canonical ingestion, campaign or per-post knowledge publication.

Construct the store with a trusted local `OfflineXRoute(path)`, an explicit `operators=frozenset({...})`, and optionally a trusted UTC clock. Source/provider/model calls are absent. The route is synthetic test evidence and establishes no live X readiness or human usefulness validation. No daemon or scheduler is installed: an operator explicitly invokes each poll.

### Topic and policy operations

`set_policy(document, human_operator, expected_version=...)` creates or updates current source permission. The exact fields are `context_id`, boolean `can_process`, `can_display`, `can_retain`, `revoked`, and nullable timezone-aware `expires_at`. New policies use version 0; changes require the exact current version. Policies are configured separately from topic edits; saving a topic grants no permission.

`configure(topic_key, settings, human_operator, expected_version=..., request_id=...)` saves immutable topic revisions. New topics use version 0. Keys are 1–80 lowercase letters/digits/underscore/hyphen; queries are 1–200 characters of literal case-insensitive text. Exact settings are:

| Setting | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `query` | Literal post text match; no inferred topic, language or sentiment |
| `cadence_minutes` | Integer 1–1,440; UTC epoch-aligned explicit poll boundaries |
| `window_minutes` | Integer 1–10,080; inclusive publication start, exclusive end |
| `max_records` | Integer 1–100 unique source captures admitted per window |
| `retention_hours` | Integer 1–720; local maximum copied-body lifetime |
| `source_policy_id` | Existing current source permission context |

Identical configuration requests reopen their saved revision even after edits; changed actor/input conflicts and stale expected versions fail. Topics are separate from scheduled KB keywords, even if their query text overlaps. At most 50 topics, 100 revisions per topic, 50 source policy contexts and 20,000 native withdrawal identities are retained per workspace. These finite audit caps reject further additions; the implementation does not silently delete history or recycle identities.

### Poll identity, source times and coverage

`poll(topic_key, window_end, human_operator)` takes an explicit timezone-aware end. New windows must be within the preceding seven days, no later than the trusted clock, and aligned to the configured cadence from the UTC epoch. The window starts `window_minutes` earlier. A topic plus normalized end is one logical poll across configuration changes/restarts/concurrent retries: an existing window reopens before consulting the new configuration or fixture. A new end uses the then-current immutable topic revision. Collection/retries both require the current trusted operator role. Failed input, source-time, file or identity validation commits no window.

The route reads exactly one regular local JSON file, at most 262,144 bytes and 200 input records, with one bounded file-read request and no network access. The top-level fields are `coverage` (`complete`, `partial` or `missing`) and `records`; `missing` requires an empty list. Each record has exactly `capture_id`, digit-string X `native_id` (1–24 digits), nullable `text` (at most 4,096 characters), nullable timezone-aware `published_at`, `captured_at`, `metric_measured_at`, and a `metrics` object. Native source references are canonical `https://x.com/i/status/ID` pointers, not verified availability. Example: [recent X fixture](../examples/monitoring/recent-x.json).

Capture, publication, metric measurement and local import times remain distinct. Known capture/measurement times cannot be in the future; publication cannot follow its capture and measurement cannot follow a known capture. Unknown publication or text makes window/query eligibility unassessable and excludes that row; unknown capture is retained visibly without a freshness claim. Missing measurements/counts remain unknown, never zero. Original source capture identity is native post plus original capture time, independently of mutable text and fixture `capture_id` labels. With unknown capture, available original publication/measurement times form a conservative identity and establish no refresh. Changed values within that identity fail; genuinely later source captures remain separate observations. Reuse preserves the first import/acquisition metadata, body tombstone and retention deadline; a later import cannot manufacture source freshness.

Selection deduplicates source captures before a stable native/capture ordering and configured record cap. Windows retain original configuration, human query, requested bounds, scanned/selected/unassessable/duplicate/withdrawn counts, truncation, field completeness and declared fixture coverage. Unknown eligibility, capture time, withdrawn input or truncation makes an otherwise complete sample partial. `complete` means only that the supplied local fixture scan completed: market coverage stays `unknown`, `baseline=null`, and empty results establish no market absence or quantitative/semantic change. Acquisition retains route/version, synthetic classification, exact file SHA-256/bytes/request count, original policy version, sampling method `literal-query-capture-order-v1`, record schema `offline-x-record-v1`, and hard file record/byte limits; it creates no task receipt or billing claim.

Metrics have X-specific meanings: `like_count` is public likes, `repost_count` public reposts, `reply_count` public replies and `view_count` reported views at `metric_measured_at`. They are nonnegative integers up to 10^15, not unique audience, sentiment, sales or cross-source comparable engagement. No combined engagement metric is synthesized.

### Reopen and lifecycle

`list_topics(actor)`, `read_topic(key, actor)`, `read_window(id, actor)`, `timeline(key, actor, limit=50, offset=0)` and `read_observation(id, actor)` reopen workspace-scoped data after restart without the fixture route. Window reads pin original membership and return at most 100 observations. The topic view shows the latest 100 windows and all bounded topic revisions; older windows reopen by ID. Timeline pages contain 1–100 distinct captures, ordered by original capture (unknown last) then stable observation ID; offset is bounded by 20,000 and `has_more` is explicit. A topic retains at most 1,000 windows and the workspace at most 20,000 observations, including tombstones. Further new windows/captures fail atomically at those finite caps. Repeated window membership is not a new source post or refresh.

Current process/display/retention policy is checked under the same workspace lock as collection and policy writes. Process and display reads use their corresponding permission independently; neither bypasses retention. Retained bodies contain only text and original metric values. On source retention denial, revocation, policy expiry or local expiry, synchronous reconciliation permanently removes those bodies while keeping source-free lineage/times/missingness and original collection coverage. Permission recovery, retries or renamed fixture labels cannot restore them. Reads never substitute later evidence into a historical window.

The local body deadline is the earlier of original known capture and first import plus the configured retention duration. With unknown capture, first import bounds local storage only and grants no recency claim. Reuse can shorten, never extend this deadline. `withdraw(native_id, human_operator)` permanently removes all current copied bodies for that native post and excludes future captures, retaining an attributable native tombstone. Policy mutations, new polls and policy-aware reads reconcile synchronously; no background deletion or universal file cleanup is claimed. The caller-supplied local fixture remains an external input file, not a retained product copy.

The [local browser workflow](local-review.md#bounded-offline-recent-x-topics) exposes topic, window and capture views; policy writes/withdrawal remain trusted Python operations. `purpose="display"` is the default on window/timeline/detail; trusted process reads explicitly select `purpose="process"`. Source text is escaped and governed again on each request.

## Configured quantitative volume assessments

[`smi_monitoring.alerts`](../src/smi_monitoring/alerts/__init__.py) owns `QuantitativeAlerts(recent, threshold=VolumeThreshold(...), clock=...)`. Its `migrate()` installs the recent and alert schemas additively; `MonitoringStore.migrate()` also installs them. Detection needs only bounded recent evidence, independently of catalogs, canonical entities, KB publications, campaigns, models or acquisition tasks. The trusted clock records the assessment time, independently of original source/window times.

`VolumeThreshold` requires explicit integer `minimum_baseline_posts`, `minimum_increase_posts` and `minimum_ratio`; the first two are 1–100 and the ratio is 2–100. There are no hidden production defaults. These are operator-selected signal parameters, not statistically calibrated probabilities or requirements-fixed values. `detect(detection_window_id, baseline_window_id_or_none, human_operator, request_id=...)` records method `distinct_native_post_volume` / `distinct-post-volume-v1`, the exact configuration, metric meaning, synthetic uncertainty, explicit original windows, outcome, limitation codes, actor/kind and time. Threshold crossing means both the absolute increase and configured multiplicative ratio are met. It is an increase signal in the supplied synthetic sample, not independent statistical significance or proof of market growth. Engagement metrics are not used; their missingness does not invalidate otherwise complete volume coverage.

Each window counts distinct native X posts, regardless of repeated captures. Samples require the same topic, exact query, cadence, source-policy context, acquisition route/version/synthetic classification, record schema, sampling method and actual record/file limits. Durations must be equal, positive and nonoverlapping, with baseline ending no later than detection start. A native identity appearing in both disjoint windows discloses incompatible publication times. Neither topic edits nor current configuration replace the original settings. A legacy acquisition lacking recorded sampling is unassessable. Fixture SHA/bytes and policy-version numbers may differ: they identify actual inputs/authority history rather than a change in metric definition. Complete fixture scan coverage still leaves market coverage unknown.

Absent baseline, partial/missing collection, truncation, unknown eligibility/capture times, incompatible sampling/scope/windows or unavailable source support save `not_assessable` with visible limitations and no alert. A baseline below the configured minimum, including zero, has no numerical ratio. A complete empty detection window can be a scoped zero only with currently permitted complete support and an adequate baseline; it creates no increase alert. Below-threshold valid comparisons save `no_change`. A supported crossing saves `significant_change` plus one retained alert and durable local notification in the same PostgreSQL transaction.

Pair/window/method/configuration uniqueness prevents duplicate alerts across new request IDs. Exact request retries also pin actor/kind/workspace and reject changed inputs/configuration; the first decision for a saved pair is never silently reevaluated after policy changes. At most 5,000 assessments and 20,000 detection request receipts are retained per workspace; caps fail atomically. `history(actor)` returns the latest 100 assessments, including limitations/no-change. `read_assessment(id, actor)` and `read_alert(id, actor)` reopen exact older records after restart without a route or threshold. `notifications(actor)` returns the latest 100 durable notification identities/messages/alert links; an uninstalled optional alert schema yields no notifications so existing task-only inboxes continue to work. Notifications are local and require no external transport.

The alert owner stores source-free window references, method/configuration, outcome/limitation codes and identity/attribution/time only. It stores no caption, copied metric or numerical-comparison body. Original window membership and acquisition/coverage remain retained by the recent owner. Displayed counts/ratios/evidence reopen that exact support under current process, display and retention permissions. If any support is denied, withdrawn or expired, the entire numerical comparison is withheld, while historical outcome/alert/notification IDs and current unavailability remain visible. Retention loss/expiry/withdrawal permanently purges original bodies; retries, permission recovery, fixture aliases and later captures cannot reconstruct a purged claim. There is no cached alert explanation to bypass source policy.

`RecentMonitoring.read_window_in_transaction(connection, id, actor, purpose=...)` is the concrete local composition interface: the caller owns a connection from the same database and its commit/rollback, while recent evidence owns its reconciliation, SQL and workspace policy lock. It returns original configuration and current purpose permissions even for empty windows; the lock lasts through dependent assessment/alert commit. No transaction spans external I/O. `require_operator(actor)` checks trusted operator mutation authority. Alert storage issues no private recent/task SQL; the browser composes its public notifications beside task completion events. See [ADR 0019](adr/0019-source-free-quantitative-alert-history.md).

The [browser contract](local-review.md#quantitative-x-assessments-and-alert-inbox) exposes explicit window selection, assessment/alert history and the inbox. Detection has no operation that creates a campaign, broadens a topic, launches external search or submits acquisition. Source/model/live execution, human language quality and marketer usefulness are not established by these offline software checks.

## Bounded provisional quality-controversy assessments

[`smi_monitoring.semantic`](../src/smi_monitoring/semantic/__init__.py) owns `SemanticAlerts(recent, threshold=ControversyThreshold(...), model_route=..., model_transport=..., model_credential=..., clock=...)`. `migrate()` installs recent evidence, shared costs and its own assessment/request/alert tables; `MonitoringStore.migrate()` composes these additive migrations. This owner consumes public recent-policy and shared Responses/accounting ports; it has no task, acquisition, campaign, KB-explanation or external-research interface. The quantitative volume method stays independent.

`detect(window_id, human_operator, request_id=...)` assesses one exact original window with method `possible_quality_controversy` / `bounded-quality-controversy-v1` and output schema `exact-post-quality-position-v1`. There is **no baseline**: saved comparison context always records `baseline_window_id=null`, `change_status=not_assessed` and the limitation `no_baseline_no_change_claim`. A result is not an emerging controversy or semantic shift claim. The configured topic is a literal retrieval scope, not a verified product identity or shared disputed proposition.

The method supplies every retained text from that window, with exact observation/native identity, source reference, original capture/publication/import times, frozen topic/query/revision/window, original acquisition and coverage. It excludes unused metric values. It accepts at most **20 captures**, without silent selection or text truncation, and at most **131,072 UTF-8 bytes** of context plus the route's whole-request bound. Larger, empty, incomplete or currently process/display/retention-denied windows save an explicit no-call limitation. Complete local fixture coverage leaves market coverage unknown. Missing source times and metric fields retain their original unknowns; importing later establishes no new source recency.

The model returns one judgment for every exact supplied observation, consisting only of its ID, position `quality_concern`, `quality_counterposition` or `unresolved`, and a zero-based source-text span `[start,end)` of 1–300 Unicode code points for non-unresolved positions. Unresolved positions require null offsets. Independent validation rejects missing/duplicate/unknown IDs, other labels, out-of-bounds or boolean offsets, contradictory labels for captures of one native post, extra fields/authority/prose, tools, refusal and malformed output. Quotations are resolved from governed source text; a valid span establishes exact quotation membership, **not semantic entailment or truth**. Source text and context are untrusted data and confer no roles, tool authority, approval, topic changes, spending configuration or research capability.

`ControversyThreshold(minimum_concern_posts, minimum_counterposition_posts)` requires explicit integers of 1–20 with a sum at most 20; there are no production defaults. The server counts distinct native posts per position, so repeated captures do not multiply support. Meeting both thresholds saves `possible_controversy`, one retained semantic alert and one unique local notification identity atomically with successful finalization. A valid below-threshold result is `no_signal`; that means only that this method found no configured signal in this sample. Semantic positions remain **provisional automated interpretations**, distinct from a numerical measurement, independently verified sentiment/controversy, statistical significance, causal claims, Japanese-original accuracy or human usefulness. Positions may concern different product-quality propositions. This bounded method does not explain why a finding matters using arbitrary generated factual prose. See [ADR 0020](adr/0020-bounded-semantic-positions-full-input-policy.md).

Before any external I/O, the owner commits a source-free claim with the exact original window, all prepared model-input capture IDs and SHA-256 context digest, method/schema/threshold/model/bounds, actor/kind and time. Exact request retries bind actor/workspace and configuration; changed reuse conflicts. New requests for the same configured window reopen its first result. Each workspace retains at most **5,000 assessments** and **20,000 request receipts**, with atomic cap failure. A missing model route/credential/enablement, invalid bounds, admission refusal, invalid/incomplete output, uncertain transport or failed meter is visible without an alert. Once claimed, interruption or failed final local commit never silently redispatches, even if usage already settled. A fresh configuration/window creates explicit new work; it is not recovery of a missing response.

Calls reuse the installed disabled-by-default shared Responses transport, exact verified provider snapshot contract, one-call/no-tools bounds and shared cost admission. No provider/rate/default enablement is introduced. The cost actor is the initiating human operator; generated authorship is separately automation. A returned invalid/incomplete semantic result can still consume final metered usage. Lost response or invalid final usage retains outstanding liability and no-reissue identity. Attribution/usage records contain no prompt, excerpt, raw response, generated prose or credential. Existing provider configuration requires its established concrete readiness gates; default software verification uses counted simulated I/O only.

The admission `dispatch_guard(connection)` reopens the **full exact input**, including unresolved text and all source context that influenced classification, after accounting contention and before dispatch commit. The public recent owner holds its policy lock through that commit; model I/O occurs after the transaction closes. Finalization repeats the full-input digest and current process/display/retention check under the lock through judgment/alert commit. Lost or changed input makes the result `stale` without an alert while retaining real usage. No database transaction spans model I/O.

`read_assessment(id, actor)`, `read_alert(id, actor)`, `history(actor)` and `notifications(actor)` reopen without a route, credential or threshold. History and notifications show the latest **100 per method**; exact older IDs remain readable. Retained judgment rows contain only exact IDs, fixed position labels and span offsets, never copied excerpts or a generated factual narrative. Current quotations and original window bodies come from policy-aware recent reads. Observed unavailability of **any** original input permanently clears all dependent judgments and marks `support_purged`; subsequent permission recovery, retries, fixture aliases and newer captures cannot reconstruct them. Original historical outcome, alert/notification identity, window/method/input/cost lineage remain inspectable as unavailable history. Current process denial also withholds source text even where display remains allowed. Reconciliation runs synchronously on these public operations; no background deletion guarantee is claimed.

Notifications share the public local alert shape but identify `kind=semantic` and use distinct semantic routes. Their fixed messages say provisional possible controversy or unavailable derived finding, never copied source wording or a numerical spike. The browser composes them alongside volume alerts and task completions through public readers, without fake task events. See [the browser contract](local-review.md#provisional-semantic-x-assessments-and-alerts). No live source/model execution, original-language semantic quality or marketer usefulness is established by offline tests.

## Accepted future X anomaly path

Semantic shift, broader semantic methods, KB-based explanation, live X collection, a recurring scheduler and selective KB promotion remain unimplemented. Full KB modeling of every recent post is not required before the installed quantitative and provisional semantic detection. [ADR 0004](adr/0004-lightweight-monitoring-evidence.md) records the separate evidence lifecycle.

Future anomaly investigation uses existing KB context only, without additional external searches, scope expansion or campaign creation. Any future copied alert explanation must follow the same source policy and retention lifecycle; promotion will use normal ingestion/publication with lineage rather than making the entire feed a KB publication. The installed topic/window/timeline path grants none of those future actions.
