# Campaign decisions

Own campaign briefs, comparison drafts and attributed automated and human shortlist revisions. Public entry points are `CampaignStore`, `Brief`, `Criterion` and `Selection`. Knowledge and ingestion keep their existing ownership; use their public readers for validation and evidence access.

`require_current_brief` validates and share-locks an exact current workspace brief in the caller transaction; dependent admission joins that transaction. Human actions start/revise campaigns and save human selections; `recommend` requires trusted automation and saves a distinct `generated` revision without human review. Automation may prepare comparisons only for an existing human-initiated campaign. Actor context is trusted local application context, not client authentication. Confirmation requires supported relevance, acknowledged unknowns and current brief/selection versions.

Keep historical membership/order, producers and revision IDs immutable. Request IDs identify retries; changed request bodies conflict. A new request makes a successor revision. Rule-based comparisons retain exact observation/assertion/interpretation dependencies and explicit coverage rather than global creator scores.

Save original human prose and the executable structured-profile snapshot atomically with each new brief. Preserve criterion IDs for unchanged needs; pin profile identity to its exact brief/version. Profile versions describe installed behavior: current ordering is unranked and coverage descriptive. Legacy null profiles stay unrecorded; readers must not synthesize historical versions. Prose and structured needs are human input, never source facts. Keep the profile's criteria consistent with the exact persisted brief; current comparisons reopen that pinned profile.

Source-dependent comparison bodies and automated or human reasons inherit all cited support's policy. Denied or superseded support is withheld; retention withdrawal purges copied bodies/reasons. Permitted account references and decision metadata remain. Do not rebuild a purged explanation from other stores.

Core criteria and identities remain category/market independent. Product/language semantics must match the knowledge method actually used; literal script evidence cannot become audience or sales evidence. Update `docs/campaigns.md`, architecture and the physical ERD with behavior/schema changes.

`interpret_criteria` owns one durable attempt for an exact current human brief and immutable retry identity. Commit the claim before simulated I/O; reopen it on retries without redispatch, including interrupted/uncertain outcomes. Persist only validated generated data and sanitized provenance. Recheck current brief before making a result executable, and preserve failed/stale outcomes visibly. The interpretation submodule owns Responses mapping/validation; accounting retains all cost SQL. Generated additions preserve the human profile unchanged. `prepare_comparison(..., interpretation_id=...)` explicitly selects a succeeded interpretation for that same brief; pin it on the draft and keep historical reads exact.

`prepare_current_comparison` owns the additional bounded current-KB path. Compose public knowledge search/account-support/status interfaces in the same transaction as the comparison commit; knowledge owns publication-frontier locking and ingestion owns source/derivation locking. Preserve legacy frozen comparison behavior. Stable platform/namespace/native account identity plus one permitted published observation is sufficient; incomplete/metric-only profiles stay eligible. Unsupported references remain discovery gaps. Pin actual support manifests, source dates, selected freshness, exact brief/profile and actual unranked execution version; never refresh historical membership/order/explanations. Apply required exclusions and defer/acknowledge policies visibly without converting caption markers into audience/contracts/sales facts. Preparation performs no acquisition, model assessment or shortlist save. `shortlist_support` exposes current policy with exact dependencies in a caller transaction for downstream note cleanup, including nullable field-assertion links on metric-only support.

`recommend` composes `prepare_current_comparison(..., connection=...)` and shortlist persistence in one transaction, retaining campaign/publication/source locks through commit. The outer revision owns retries and a fresh server-created nested comparison identity prevents client request collisions. Exact retries reopen before reassessment; changed body/actor conflicts and new requests check expected brief/shortlist heads. Select only `eligible_with_explicit_gaps` candidates in their comparison order; save empty outcomes explicitly with source-free selection counts/reason. Pin actual automation producer, exact brief/profile/method, positions, rationale, gaps and support. Deterministic positions are unranked; keep `acknowledged_unknowns` empty and attribute reasons as automated. Preserve legacy human status/attribution and leave historical method/order/summary/positions null. Human save/confirmation remains a separate human-only operation. Do not reconstruct policy-purged explanations on retry.

`recommend_ranked` adds one durable ranking claim/comparison before provider I/O, without holding a transaction across that I/O. Deterministic eligibility runs first. The `ranking` submodule validates one exact candidate permutation and constrained candidate/criterion-scoped reasons; `responses` shares concrete transport/admission/usage handling with criteria interpretation. Pin exact eligible inputs on candidate rows and inherit their existing support cleanup for inputs/judgments/reasons. Final publication locks/rechecks actual support, source policy and both heads. Save valid model order automatically with actual automation and exact method/profile/schema/model; empty batches save a truthful no-call outcome. Failed/stale/uncertain attempts have no successful fallback. Outer claims own retries and server-created nested IDs; interrupted attempts cannot reissue or admit another in-flight generation. Current reads expose source-free attempt metadata and only permitted governed copies. Update the [ranking contract](../../docs/campaigns.md#bounded-supported-llm-ranking) and physical ERD for persistence changes.

`save_shortlist(..., source_shortlist_id=...)` owns optional ordered human curation of an exact current source revision from the same comparison/campaign. Tuple order becomes zero-based positions with a distinct human method/order; omission preserves legacy save digests and unrecorded historical order. Recheck the source and brief heads under the campaign lock after exact retry reopening. Keep human draft curation distinct from actual confirmation; link the original deterministic/model recommendation without changing its producer, order, support or review state. Curation performs no model/source I/O. Unavailable support permits content-free membership only; copied/submitted reasons inherit every candidate dependency.
