## Installed behavior and interfaces

- `UsageAccounting.reconcile_usage` is a human-operator public boundary for one identified final synthetic measurement and exact named outstanding portions of an operation: its dispatched admission, declaration entries and unknown/failure entries. Final `succeeded`/`failed` outcome, original rate/usage time, source-free final-report evidence/provenance and reconciliation time are explicit. Failure alone never supplies zero; an uncharged failure needs an explicit final zero measurement. There is no automatic expiration, ambiguous-outcome release or bounded write-off policy.
- Final usage and all named resolutions commit together under the same scope lock as admission. New measurements retain exact original rate/currency/period and record actual overage truthfully; actual reporting is not admission. A pre-existing exactly equal usage entry can be linked without another amount or recorder rewrite. Independent usage remains additive and does not silently resolve a declaration. Original declarations and uncertainty/failure entries remain append-only history; current totals count only unresolved liabilities.
- Workspace-global reconciliation identities are idempotent after restart and reject changed reuse. Each final bill is allocated once; changed facts and overlapping/conflicting targets reject without adding a second actual or releasing an unrelated portion. Canonical target lists identify exact operation/kind membership. Final attestation time cannot predate its measurement or named target observations.
- A reconciled reservation remains settled. A retry never obtains another transport grant from that operation, even for a confirmed uncharged failure. When an original callback races final reconciliation, equal units reuse the final charge; different units raise a visible measurement conflict without a second charge. Existing caller-supplied task transactions still fence the actual response/effect commit after session loss.
- `UsageAccounting.recover_meter` separately records a human health-check attestation for exact already-reconciled failures. Its own stable identity preserves replay/conflict handling and original operator/evidence/time. Final cost accounting, increased ceiling or task retry alone cannot restore meter health. New/unnamed failures continue to block the whole metered scope; recovery neither releases liability nor reclassifies a scope or changes a dispatch grant.
- A late final report above its declared admission bound itself records a stable expected-meter failure. That report resolves the derived failure's cost portion while its health state remains failed pending explicit recovery. All original failure observations remain; a recovery may explicitly cover their exact group. Final accounting can therefore be complete while health still blocks work.
- `read` exposes original entries, final reconciliations, recovery attestations, exact unresolved identities and current limits. Scope allowance includes actual over-ceiling amount. Limits distinguish original declared bounds and their declared/unverified/within/violated state, final-billing state, meter health and the bounded-synthetic enforcement premise. Cost detail and relevant task detail reopen this current summary independently of saved task progress, with the explicit delayed/unbounded-charge zero-overshoot limit. Every amount remains synthetic; no commercial charge or new provider is enabled.

Two additive tables, `cost_reconciliations` and `cost_meter_recoveries`, preserve the immutable audit. Migration is idempotent and task migration continues composing the independent accounting migration. Accounting imports no task code or task-table SQL. Task attribution still uses the existing injected public resolver, and task sessions/publication ownership receipts retain their existing independent boundaries. Documentation includes the authoritative cost contract, architecture, physical ERD, dictionary, glossary, current coverage and local operator behavior; ADR 0007 records final-evidence and separate-health trade-offs, with ADR 0006 linked forward. Updated owner instructions retain the existing relative `CLAUDE.md -> AGENTS.md` symlinks.

## Limits and extension notes

- Evidence and health checks are explicit trusted human assertions for the established synthetic meter, not independent verification of a commercial provider invoice or its health. Existing source-free provenance conventions apply. No live source/provider/model call, external research, external message, push or PR occurred.
- This slice settles supported final evidence only. An unresolved/ambiguous operation keeps its commitment; no timeout, forecast fallback, automatic write-off, partial-liability policy or provider-side response/retry operation is installed. A future policy needs its own explicit bounded contract and audit.
- Final measurement identities represent one report covering the exact named group. Callers must use this final boundary rather than recording a purported replacement as another independent additive usage portion. Aggregate measurements and commitments are never combined across currencies/rates/periods.
- Accounting resolution cannot recover an unavailable response or create usable knowledge. Tasks can remain waiting with their settled operation; existing evidence, authority, session fencing and receipt-backed publication recovery remain required. Reconciliation never grants another call for that operation.
- Exact allowance is enforced only under the trusted bounded fake-service premise. Late/unrecorded external spending and contract-violating outcomes can exceed a ceiling. Bounds, meter failures, final-report lag/state and this limit remain visible; no zero-overshoot or commercial-spend guarantee is asserted.
- The newest-100 operation list remains a presentation bound. Scope allowance and reconciliation retain all historical liabilities. There is no blocker for ticket 11. Software tests do not establish human language or marketer-usefulness quality.
