# Retain quantitative alert lineage without copied source claims

Status: accepted

Quantitative assessment pins its explicit frozen detection/baseline windows, configured method and historical outcome; alert and local notification identities commit with the assessment. It retains no separate caption, metric or numerical-comparison body. Current counts and evidence reopen through the recent evidence owner, which holds its current-policy lock through the dependent local commit. This trades independently cached explanations for a single governed source lifecycle: withdrawal can withhold the complete claim without leaving alert copies to purge or allowing retries to reconstruct them.

A configured distinct-native-post volume threshold compares only complete, equal-duration, nonoverlapping samples with the same topic/query, source context, cadence, acquisition route/version, record schema, sampling method and limits. Repeated captures are one post. Missing/partial coverage, unavailable support and inadequate baselines produce retained limitations instead of inferred zero activity. This signal is an operator-selected sample threshold, not independent statistical significance or proof of market change. Semantic methods and KB explanation remain separate future work.

Notifications belong to the alert owner and retain a source-free identity with the alert; the browser composes them alongside task completions through public readers. Modeling a detection as an acquisition task would invent execution/publication history and couple unrelated ownership. Neither assessment nor notification grants acquisition, scope expansion or campaign authority.
