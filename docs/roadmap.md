# Product direction and readiness

The product destination is a shared intelligence foundation for brand and agency marketers: observe accessible markets, investigate signals, compare creators, plan campaigns, measure outcomes and reuse learning. Beauty in South Korea and Japan is the first proving domain, using one multilingual core model with market-specific labels, channels, taxonomies and effective-dated policies. The complete [proposal](../POPOSAL.md) and [accepted requirements](functional-requirements.md) remain authoritative.

## Current system

The local Python/PostgreSQL application implements frozen evidence import, replay/resume, source-policy reads, Brand/Product catalogs, provisional caption rules, atomic publications and bounded literal queries. Browser/CLI workflows persist campaign briefs/criteria, deterministic or bounded supported model recommendations and automatic shortlists, optional human curation, independent snapshot investigations, proposed measurement plans and decision notes.

Tasks support exact human-approved assistant enrichment, automatic campaign coverage discovery and configured keyword schedules, with bounded execution/recovery, partial outcomes, local completion inbox, shared daily unique-post admission and capture-based offline avoidance. Bounded extractive KB answers, recent X collection, configured sample-volume alerts, provisional quality-controversy alerts, existing-KB context, selected promotion, graph traversal, task lineage and quality packets are installed.

These are bounded local implementations. Compose runs only PostgreSQL; the browser, task worker and monitoring worker are explicit processes/operations. Schedule ticks acknowledge work rather than start a consumer. Optional model/live transports require explicit trusted configuration; software tests simulate external I/O. Full operational A/B/C delivery still requires the readiness evidence and broader capabilities below.

## Architecture and growth choices

Keep canonical evidence authoritative for KB answers and decisions, with search/vector/graph/analytics as rebuildable projections. Preserve observed, derived and human assertions, conflict history, exact support, relevant times, metric/method versions, uncertainty and current permission. Reconcile missed updates/source corrections and propagate withdrawal/deletion to every retained copy and rebuild. The separate bounded X path remains valid before full KB publication.

The proposal's technology choices are **target options**, selected when measured needs justify them: a Python/FastAPI service with workers/managed queue/scheduler; managed PostgreSQL and transactional outbox; policy-controlled encrypted object storage; PostgreSQL retrieval and useful `pgvector` embeddings; Parquet/DuckDB analytics and versioned Python/SQL methods; code-native quality/health, tenant authorization, secrets and OpenTelemetry. Durable workflows, CDC/streaming, dedicated Korean/Japanese search/vector/graph systems, warehouses/model serving and specialized governance tools follow demonstrated latency, recall, replay, workload, cost or ownership needs. They are not the current deployed stack or commitments to microservices or a graph database.

Every consequential future output must state its decision, observed population, market/source coverage, time window, metric/method versions, evidence, freshness, uncertainty and limits. The approximate all-in operating target remains planning context; no price, currency, budget amount, retention duration, throughput or accuracy claim is invented from it. Actual derivable costs receive configured controls; unmeterable portions remain explicit forecasts.

The platform supports internal human decisions. Creator outreach, publishing, media buying, creator contracting and external campaign activation remain outside its product boundary.
